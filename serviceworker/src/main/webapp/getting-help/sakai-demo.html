Request Support

As instructors encounter bugs or other support issues on Sakai Demo, they are strongly encouraged to notify the PLU Sakai Support Team (sakai@plu.edu) so that these issues might be identified and resolved prior to PLU Sakai's planned upgrade in August. As bug fixes are made ready, the Sakai Support Team will release them on Sakai Demo, which will be briefly out of service each night in order to incorporate such updates.

Though Sakai help and support documentation for Sakai 12 during this time will still be incrementally under development before the August upgrade, the Sakai Support Team aims to smooth this transition to Sakai 12 as much as possible, including the resolution of Sakai 12 bugs prior to August.
